title: "Wall Street"
  
---
<div class="poem"> 
       <it>to Howard Piggee</it>


<br><br><br>

I am standing<br>
on a corner on Wall Street<br>
in New York<br>
waiting for her<br>
though I know she will never come<br>
my body is a tiny ant<br>
standing among millions of giants<br>
the city overcrowded<br>
the air overpopulated<br>
there is no room, not even for my soul<br>
yellow taxis passing by<br>
like peaches in my back yard<br>
yellow taxis passing by<br>
and shouting in unison <br>
how beautiful America is<br>
how beautiful America is<br>
oh, and how razor sharp!

</div>
