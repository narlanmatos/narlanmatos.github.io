# Website Credits

This website was created to document, preserve, and present the literary work of **Narlan Matos** for an international audience. 
It brings together original poetry, translations, books, recordings, scholarship, and bibliographic information from numerous published sources.

The website is an ongoing archival project. New publications, translations, recordings, and related materials will be added as they become available.

## Poetry and Translations

Translations presented on this website remain the work of their respective translators and are credited on each poem, book, or publication page.

Previously unpublished translations are reproduced with the permission of the translators or their authorized representatives.
